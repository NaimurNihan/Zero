Text SRT Cleaner Skill
Clean messy raw transcripts by removing duplicate time descriptions and outputting a clean, readable timestamp + text format.

What Problem This Solves
Raw transcripts (like YouTube auto-captions) often look like this:
0:10 10 seconds Some text here
1:07 1 minute, 7 seconds More text here
2:34 2 minutes, 34 seconds Even more text
The timestamp appears twice — once as 0:10 and again as 10 seconds or 1 minute, 7 seconds. This skill removes the duplicate and keeps only the clean short form.

Input Format
User will paste raw transcript lines like:
0:00 Hello everyone, this is a video about...
0:10 10 seconds A long time ago in a village...
1:07 1 minute, 7 seconds The man's name is Tang...
2:34 2 minutes, 34 seconds He grabs the infant...
15:25 15 minutes, 25 seconds So what do you think...

The 3 Rules — Follow ALL strictly
✅ Rule 1 – Remove Duplicate Time Description

After the short timestamp (e.g. 0:10), if a spoken time description follows (e.g. 10 seconds, 1 minute, 7 seconds, 2 minutes, 34 seconds), remove it completely
Keep ONLY the short timestamp at the start

Patterns to remove:
Remove ThisKeep This10 seconds—1 minute, 7 seconds—2 minutes, 34 seconds—15 minutes, 25 seconds—Any variation of "X seconds", "X minute(s), X seconds"—

✅ Rule 2 – Output Format
Every line must follow this exact format:
0:10 - Text starts here
1:07 - Text starts here

Short timestamp first
Then - (space dash space)
Then the clean text


✅ Rule 3 – Keep Text Exactly As-Is

Do NOT translate the text
Do NOT change any words
Do NOT merge lines
Do NOT split lines
Only remove the duplicate time description — nothing else


Output Format
0:00 - Hello everyone, this is a video about...
0:10 - A long time ago in a village...
1:07 - The man's name is Tang...
2:34 - He grabs the infant...
15:25 - So what do you think...
